Questions before the first request

Clear scope. Clear ownership.

Which tools and data sources do you support?+

We primarily work with Microsoft Power BI and Fabric, and connect common commerce, advertising, CRM, finance, ERP, spreadsheet, database, and API-based systems. We confirm feasibility during discovery.

How quickly will we see something useful?+

You will see progress in short review cycles. Delivery time depends on data access, quality, and complexity. A clean, focused first use case can move quickly; larger models are delivered in stages.

Who owns the dashboards, models, and code?+

You do. Work is built in or transferred to your environment, with documentation appropriate to the engagement. Your data and operating knowledge remain under your control.

How do you protect our data?+

We use least-privilege access, role-based permissions, secure connections, and your existing Microsoft controls. The exact security model is agreed before any production data is accessed.

Do we need Power BI or Fabric licenses?+

Licensing depends on your users, sharing model, and technical architecture. We help identify the appropriate setup, but Microsoft licenses are purchased separately from official channels.

What does “one active priority” mean?+

You can maintain a backlog of requests. We focus the team on the highest-priority item, review progress with you, then move to the next approved request. This keeps delivery visible and avoids fragmented work.
